The repository SHA256SUMS.json checks all delivered files except itself.
PAYLOADS.json and INPUT_MEMBERS.csv.gz describe the compressed archives and
their original members. Verification reports generated by the commands are
written only beneath the ignored outputs directory.

## Scope of validation

This release was checked by restoring and replaying saved records, rebuilding
statistics and figures, checking the selected raw arrays, and running existing
tests and source diagnostics. The full training, validation and test simulation
campaigns were not rerun. The 71 arrays are a deterministic subset, not a random
sample or a validation of all 120,804 arrays in the complete collection.

The trace check recomputes scalars from stored arrays and sums stored physical
interval efforts. It does not independently recompute fine-step physical forces,
all state/attitude discrepancies, event equivalence, numerical qualification
thresholds, reference convergence or physical correctness. The three RISE
production-step failures and their refined-step results remain as reported.
Source-condition reconstruction limits and blocked PX4 hypothesis families
remain unchanged.

A new dependency installation, non-Windows execution, all optional baseline
figure/smoke entry points, and a new full scientific campaign were not tested
in this release check. Archive integrity and statistical replay do not establish
hardware validity or universal controller performance.
